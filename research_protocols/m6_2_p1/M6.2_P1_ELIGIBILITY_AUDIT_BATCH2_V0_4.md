## 4. QuantConnect minute-bar timestamp semantics

The same bar-end timestamp convention observed in the first audit is reproduced in Batch 2:

- frozen 10:00 ET entry -> QC minute bar stamped 10:01 ET;
- frozen 12:30 ET entry -> QC minute bar stamped 12:31 ET.

The economic entry remains the minute interval beginning at the frozen timestamp; this is not a discretionary one-minute delay.

## 5. Outcome-blindness audit
