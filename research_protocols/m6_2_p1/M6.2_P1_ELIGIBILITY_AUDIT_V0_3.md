## 5. QuantConnect minute-bar timestamp semantics

The planned entry is defined as the beginning of the first executable minute at or after the frozen entry time. QuantConnect minute history records the bar using the bar end timestamp. Therefore:

- planned 10:00 ET -> observed bar timestamp 10:01 ET;
- planned 11:30 ET -> observed bar timestamp 11:31 ET;
- planned 15:20 ET -> observed bar timestamp 15:21 ET.

This is treated as expected bar-stamp semantics, not as a one-minute discretionary delay. The protocol's economic entry remains the minute interval beginning at the frozen entry time.

## 6. Outcome-blindness audit

The accepted/rejected decision uses only information needed to establish event eligibility and executable data availability. Specifically, it does not contain or inspect:

- future close returns;
- SPY or sector excess returns;
- MFE / MAE;
- subsequent high/low path;
- later analyst revisions;
- later event interpretation;
- whether the stock became a known winner or loser.

The eligibility-audited manifest preserves `outcome_fields_present=false` for every row.
