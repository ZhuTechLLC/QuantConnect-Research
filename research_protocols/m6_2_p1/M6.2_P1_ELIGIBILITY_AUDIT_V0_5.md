## 6. Price-data and entry-timestamp semantics

Both QuantConnect audits reproduce the same minute-bar end-stamp convention:

- frozen 10:00 ET entry -> first returned minute bar stamped 10:01 ET;
- frozen 11:30 ET entry -> first returned minute bar stamped 11:31 ET;
- frozen 12:30 ET entry -> first returned minute bar stamped 12:31 ET;
- frozen 15:20 ET entry -> first returned minute bar stamped 15:21 ET.

These are minute-bar end timestamps. The protocol's economic entry is the minute interval beginning at the frozen timestamp, not a discretionary one-minute delay.

## 7. Outcome-blindness and invalid platform diagnostics

Both authoritative runs persisted `Outcomes=false`, placed zero orders and were designed only to audit eligibility. Therefore QuantConnect's flat-equity return, Sharpe, alpha, information-ratio or significance diagnostics are mechanically generated no-trade diagnostics and are **not** evidence for or against H1.

No accepted/rejected decision used:

- future close returns;
- benchmark or sector excess returns;
- later high/low paths;
- MFE / MAE;
- alpha or hit rate;
- subsequent analyst revisions;
- later narrative interpretation;
- knowledge that a stock later became a famous winner or loser.

## 8. Timestamp-quality status

Mechanical eligibility is not the same as final evidentiary freeze. Rows retaining non-final timestamp-quality labels such as `VERIFIED_BEST_AVAILABLE` or other explicit provenance qualifiers remain visible in the consolidated manifest and must be hardened or explicitly dispositioned before the final P1 manifest freeze. They are not silently upgraded in V0.5.

This preserves the distinction between:

- **mechanically accepted now**, and
- **fully provenance-frozen for the final outcome study**.
