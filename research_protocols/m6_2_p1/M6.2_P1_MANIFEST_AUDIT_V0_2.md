## 7. Deterministic eligibility audit still required

For each of the 24 candidates, QuantConnect should check only information needed to decide protocol eligibility:

1. symbol/security resolution at the event date;
2. common-equity tradability and data availability;
3. scheduled entry date is a regular session;
4. first tradable minute at/after scheduled entry exists;
5. entry price >= $5;
6. prior 20 trading-day median daily dollar volume >= $20 million;
7. halt-adjusted entry if required;
8. missing-data reason if the row cannot be audited.

The eligibility audit **must not calculate or return any bar, price, return, high, low, MFE or MAE after the eligibility/entry timestamp**, except the minimal same-session search required to find the first tradable minute when the scheduled entry is halted or missing.
