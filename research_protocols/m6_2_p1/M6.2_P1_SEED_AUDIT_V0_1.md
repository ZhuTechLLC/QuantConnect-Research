## 1. What this seed is

This file is the first **outcome-blind acquisition batch** for M6.2-P1. It is not the final primary sample and it is not a backtest input yet.

The batch intentionally includes materially different event mechanisms across five frozen categories:

- E1 Clinical / Regulatory
- E2 Earnings / Guidance Reset
- E3 Major Contract / Order / Capacity
- E4 Product / Commercialization / Strategic Change
- E5 M&A / Policy / Supply-Chain Shock

No post-event return, gap performance, MFE, MAE, alpha, hit rate or later stock path was used to accept or reject any row in this seed.

## 4. Entry timestamps

Entry timestamps were assigned mechanically from the frozen protocol only:

- pre-market event -> 10:00 ET same trading day;
- after-hours event -> 10:00 ET next regular trading day;
- regular-session event -> first tradable minute at or after event timestamp + 30 minutes.

No chart, VWAP, high/low, gap size or subsequent price path was consulted.

The current seed contains:

- 9 pre-market entries;
- 5 after-hours entries;
- 1 regular-session +30-minute entry.

Before outcome generation, the trading-calendar mapping and halt status must be validated deterministically in QuantConnect.

---

## 5. Materiality decision rule used in the seed

Materiality was assessed from the event itself, not from subsequent market reaction.

Accepted candidate mechanisms include:

- pivotal Phase 3 results with direct approval/commercial implications;
- a large forward revenue/cost/guidance reset;
- withdrawal of full-year guidance caused by a change in operating conditions;
- a contract/award economically large relative to the issuer or strategically important to future backlog;
- a company-wide separation/spin-off or strategic repositioning;
- a definitive M&A agreement that directly changes target-shareholder economics.

This seed does **not** imply that all events in these mechanisms will qualify after the liquidity, duplicate, timestamp and source audits.

## 6. Required remaining audits before any backtest

Every row is currently marked:

`PENDING_QC_LIQUIDITY_AND_DUPLICATE_AUDIT`

The following checks are still mandatory:

1. **Security-type audit** — common stock, correct symbol mapping, not ETF/SPAC/OTC at event time.
2. **Price audit** — event-entry price >= $5 using PIT QuantConnect data.
3. **Liquidity audit** — prior 20-trading-day median daily dollar volume >= $20 million.
4. **Trading-calendar audit** — entry date/time maps to an actual regular trading session.
5. **Halt audit** — if halted at scheduled entry, assign first objectively tradable minute and flag the adjustment.
6. **Duplicate-issuer audit** — enforce <=2 accepted events per issuer and >=180 calendar days between independent events.
7. **Timestamp second-source audit** — especially META and PLTR.
8. **Sector/category concentration audit** — final accepted sample must comply with the frozen ceilings.

No return calculation should run until these checks complete.

## 8. Outcome firewall

Until the manifest is frozen:

- do not query post-event returns for these rows;
- do not rank candidates by known subsequent stock performance;
- do not remove a candidate because its later move was small or adverse;
- do not add a candidate because its later move became famous;
- do not use MRNA as a template for what a 'good' event should look like.

The manifest acquisition decision must remain independent from the outcome engine.
